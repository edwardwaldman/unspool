# Characters

`girl.vrm`: the owner's VRoid Studio character (VRM 0.x, 52k triangles). Used as Maya in
`why-do-you-suck-at-tests` via `vrmperson.js` (copy it into an ad's `web/` as `girl.vrm` with three-vrm and
`jsm/` from `why-tiktok-sticks/web`).

Rules for using her in ads (a study app aimed at teens):
- No shots framed on her chest or lower body; the phone close-up is the phone alone.
- Seated with legs forward, keep her legs hidden: desks get front panels, beds a blanket.
- The file's VRM licence field says commercial use: Disallow. The owner made her, so it's theirs to use, but
  re-export from VRoid with commercial use allowed to keep the file's metadata consistent.
