# Residential lease: key terms (summary for the tenant)

Term: 12 months, starting 1 September. Rent: $1,450 per month, due on the 1st. A late fee of $50 applies if rent
is received after the 5th of the month.

Security deposit: $1,450, held in a separate account. It is returned within 30 days of move-out, minus the cost
of any damage beyond normal wear and tear. The landlord must give an itemised list of deductions.

Notice: either side must give 60 days' written notice before the end of the term. Without notice, the lease
continues month to month at the same rent.

Repairs: the tenant must report needed repairs in writing. The landlord must fix urgent problems (no heat, no
water, a broken lock) within 24 hours and other repairs within 14 days.

Pets: not allowed without written permission. A pet deposit of $300 applies if permission is given.

Subletting: not allowed without the landlord's written consent.

Entry: the landlord may enter with 24 hours' notice, except in an emergency.
