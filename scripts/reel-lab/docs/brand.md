# Lumen Coffee brand guide

## Colours
- Espresso #2B1A12: primary text and the logo on light backgrounds.
- Crema #F3E3C3: the main background colour.
- Ember #E4572E: the one accent. Buttons and links only, never large areas.
- Sage #8FA98F: secondary accent for illustrations.

## Fonts
- Fraunces (bold): headlines only.
- Inter (regular and medium): body text and buttons.
- Never use more than these two fonts on one page.

## Logo
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "L".
- Minimum size: 24 px tall on screens.
- Never stretch, rotate or recolour the logo outside the palette.

## Voice
Warm, plain and short. Talk to one person. No jargon, no exclamation marks in headlines.
